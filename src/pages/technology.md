# Technology & Innovation

MedFlow Health is built on a modern, secure architecture designed for the demands of healthcare data.

## Platform Architecture

Our platform runs on a cloud-native infrastructure with:

- **HIPAA-compliant hosting** across multiple availability zones
- **End-to-end encryption** for all data in transit and at rest
- **99.99% uptime SLA** backed by redundant failover systems
- **SOC 2 Type II** certified operations

## AI-Powered Workflows

We use machine learning to make healthcare operations smarter:

### Intelligent Scheduling
Our scheduling engine analyzes historical patterns to predict appointment duration, suggest optimal time slots, and automatically fill cancellations from the waitlist.

### Clinical Decision Support
Surface relevant clinical guidelines, drug interactions, and care gaps at the point of care — without alert fatigue. Our models learn from your practice patterns to prioritize the most actionable recommendations.

### Predictive Analytics
Identify patients at risk of hospital readmission, medication non-adherence, or care plan gaps before they become problems. Proactive outreach reduces costs and improves outcomes.

## Interoperability

Healthcare data is fragmented. MedFlow bridges the gaps:

- **FHIR R4** native API for standards-based data exchange
- **HL7v2** support for legacy system integration
- **Direct messaging** for secure provider-to-provider communication
- **Public health reporting** for immunization registries, cancer registries, and syndromic surveillance

## Security & Compliance

We take data security seriously:

- Annual penetration testing by independent firms
- Role-based access control with audit logging
- Automatic session management and MFA enforcement
- Business Associate Agreements standard with all customers
- Regular security training for all MedFlow employees

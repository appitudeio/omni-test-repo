# Our Services

MedFlow Health offers a suite of integrated tools designed for modern healthcare delivery. Each module works standalone or as part of the full platform.

## Patient Portal

A clean, mobile-first interface where patients can:

- Book and manage appointments
- View lab results and imaging reports
- Message their care team directly
- Complete intake forms before visits
- Access visit summaries and care plans

## Provider Dashboard

Real-time visibility into your practice:

- **Daily Schedule View** — See your full day at a glance with patient context pre-loaded
- **Clinical Inbox** — Lab results, referral responses, and patient messages in one feed
- **Documentation** — Structured note templates that auto-populate from intake data
- **Analytics** — Track patient outcomes, appointment utilization, and revenue metrics

## Care Coordination Hub

For multi-provider teams managing complex patients:

- Shared patient timelines across specialties
- Automated referral routing with status tracking
- Discharge planning workflows
- Social determinants of health screening and resource matching

## Telehealth

Integrated video visits with:

- One-click join from the patient portal
- Screen sharing for reviewing results together
- Automatic visit documentation
- Post-visit summary sent to the patient within minutes

## Integration Services

MedFlow connects to your existing systems:

- EHR integration (Epic, Cerner, Athena, and others)
- Lab interfaces (Quest, Labcorp, in-house)
- Pharmacy networks (Surescripts)
- Insurance eligibility and claims (Availity, Change Healthcare)

# About Us

We are a scrappy band of code wizards and pixel alchemists conjuring software magic from our secret lair (okay, it's just a shared office space, but it has really good coffee).

Our mission is to help teams work more efficiently while maintaining their sanity and sense of humor. We believe productivity shouldn't come at the cost of your soul.

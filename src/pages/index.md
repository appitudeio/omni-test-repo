# 🏥 Modern Healthcare, Simplified ✨

MedFlow Health is a digital health platform that connects patients, providers, and care teams through intelligent workflow automation. We believe healthcare should be seamless, transparent, and centered on the patient.

## What We Do

We build tools that eliminate the friction in healthcare delivery:

- **Smart Scheduling** — Patients book appointments in seconds, not phone calls
- **Care Coordination** — Every provider on the care team sees the same real-time patient timeline
- **Automated Follow-ups** — Post-visit check-ins, prescription reminders, and lab result notifications happen automatically

## Why MedFlow

Healthcare is drowning in manual processes. Fax machines, phone trees, and paper forms waste hours every day for both patients and clinicians. MedFlow replaces all of that with a single connected platform.

Our customers see an average 40% reduction in administrative overhead within the first six months.
